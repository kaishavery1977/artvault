ArtVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

ArtVault ("the app") is a private digital gallery that helps you organise, analyse and protect your art collection. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

1. What we store on your device

Your vault — artworks, images, artists, documents, condition reports and collection metadata — is stored on your device first. This is your data and it stays in your control. You can export or delete it at any time.

2. Cloud features

Cloud features are optional. If you sign in, we use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and (when enabled) Cloud Storage to:

  • keep your profile and vault synchronised across your devices;
  • back up your catalogue when you choose "Auto cloud backup";
  • power public gallery links you deliberately publish.

When you publish a public gallery link, the gallery page and its images are served to anyone with the link. View counts are recorded per link. You can revoke or expire a link at any time.

3. Data we do not collect

ArtVault does not sell your data, show ads, or track you across apps or websites. We never share your vault contents with third parties for marketing. Cloud processing is handled by Firebase / Google Cloud to provide the optional sync and backup features described above, and anything you publish via a public gallery link is intentionally shared with anyone who has the link.

4. Security

Data is protected with app lock, passcode, face and fingerprint options, and cloud features use Firebase's App Check and security rules. App Check is currently active on the Android app; iOS and web builds run the same cloud features without App Check and continue to work without an attestation token. No system is perfectly secure; you are responsible for guarding access to your device and account.

5. Your choices

  • Delete any artwork, document or backup from the app at any time.
  • Revoke public gallery links from the manage-link dialog.
  • Sign out or delete your account through the app's settings.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact the app developer, Kais Havery, via the contact details shown on the app's About screen.
